01 / INPUTS

What the model sees

Prior player production and snaps, team offense, opponent production, recent positional matchup residuals, home field, indoor venue, rest, and changes in team. All derived from free data. D/ST v0.2 uses only 15 inputs: prior sacks, interceptions, fumble recoveries and points allowed; opponent offensive statistics; home field, indoor venue and rest. Its explicit positional matchup residual is excluded.

Recent games receive exponentially declining weights. Prior-season observations receive reduced weight when current-season observations exist. These starting weights are design assumptions, not discovered laws.

02 / FORECAST

Statistics before scores

Separate regularized linear models forecast scoring components for each position. Expected stat lines become Standard, Half PPR, and PPR points. D/ST points-allowed bands are averaged across empirical residual scenarios. D/ST turnovers, touchdowns, blocks and safeties use training-league averages; sacks and points allowed remain matchup-specific.

Tier separators group model means within two points of each tier’s leader. This is a display convention, not Boris Chen’s expert clustering or a statistical significance test.

03 / VALIDATION

Time moves one way

2024 weeks 5–12 fit the initial model; weeks 13–18 estimate errors. A model fit on 2024 weeks 5–18 is evaluated on 2025, with coefficients frozen while prior-week information updates.

Live coefficients are refit on 2024–25. Current-season observations update inputs, not coefficients. D/ST v0.2 settings were selected on 2024 weeks 9–12 after earlier results had been inspected. Its 2025 comparison is retrospective, not an untouched holdout.

04 / LIMITS

What it does not know

    Explicit scoring presets

    Offense: 0.04 per passing yard, 4 per passing TD, −2 per interception; 0.1 per rushing/receiving yard, 6 per rushing/receiving TD, −2 per fumble lost, 2 per two-point conversion. Receptions: 0 / 0.5 / 1.

    Kicker: 3 for a made FG below 40 yards, 4 at 40–49, 5 at 50+; 1 per PAT; −1 per missed FG/PAT. Blocked attempts are separate source fields and are not deducted by this preset.

    D/ST: 1 per sack; 2 per interception, recovered opponent fumble, safety, blocked punt/FG/PAT; 6 per defensive/special-teams TD. Scoreboard points allowed: 0→10, 1–6→7, 7–13→4, 14–20→1, 21–27→0, 28–34→−1, 35+→−4. This is not exact platform scoring.

    IDP: 1 per tackle credited as solo (including a solo tackle with another player’s assist), 0.5 per assisted tackle; 1 per tackle for loss; 3 per sack/interception; 1 per pass defended; 2 per forced/recovered fumble/safety; 6 per defensive TD. Categories stack.
