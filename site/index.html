Simple average. Explicit safeguards.

One source, one vote

Equal-weight positional rank averages, PPR only. ESPN and FFToday projections are ordered into ranks; equal projections receive tied midranks. These are labeled separately from analyst rankings. Projection sources each get one vote, not one per position or statistic. No accuracy weights without comparable evidence. Derived lists such as Boris Chen and FantasyPros ECR are not extra votes.

Outliers, not disagreements

Filtering is optional and off by default: it did not consistently improve our historical checks. It requires seven contributing analysts. Votes must sit more than max(5 ranks, 3 × 1.4826 × median absolute deviation) from the median. At most 20% of votes are removed. Both averages remain visible.

Coverage first

Main board requires three sources and 60% of selected, available sources for that position. Missing ranks never become zero. Limited coverage is optional and marked. Fewer than three sources produces no main consensus.

Honest history

Actual PPR points come from nflverse. Historical rosters match identities and retain rostered players with zero stats. Started games are excluded. Matched comparisons use identical players and weeks across all seven analysts. Rank correlation is not point-error accuracy.

Failed or wrong-week feeds are excluded, not replaced with stale rankings. K/DST consensus has scoring differences; historical PPR audit covers offensive positions. No FLEX or IDP consensus is fabricated from positional ranks.
